FREIGHT PLANNING

Plan the route
before you buy.

These are planning windows, not a final quote. Product restrictions, packing, route availability and clearance can change the final plan.

SEA

50 to 60 days after shipment

Allow about 70 to 80 days from payment through procurement, packing and delivery to the shipping harbour. Sea freight suits larger or less time-sensitive cargo.

AIR

About 2 to 4 weeks

Air freight is usually quicker, but batteries, product type, dimensions and airline acceptance can affect the route and timing.

RATES

Start with a useful range

For eligible air freight, Zimbabwe and Zambia planning ranges are US$18 to US$25 per kg. The UK, US and Australia currently use a US$10 per kg planning rate. Final charges depend on the cargo and route.

    A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

    Good questions.
    Clear answers.

    All frequently asked questions ↗
    Can you help me find a supplier in China?+

    Yes. Send the product description, quantity, destination and any specifications or photos. We can help identify suitable sourcing options and coordinate the next steps.

    Can I send an Alibaba or 1688 link?+

    Yes. You can share Alibaba, 1688, Taobao, Made-in-China or a supplier website link. Include the product options and quantity you want so the enquiry is clear.

    Can you ship machinery?+

    We can help with machinery sourcing and shipping enquiries. Share the application, output, electrical requirements, dimensions and weight. Handling and freight options depend on the machine.

    Can you ship vehicles?+

    We review vehicle enquiries individually. Share the make, model, year, condition, supplier and destination. Eligibility and documents must be confirmed before purchase or shipment.

    Can multiple orders be consolidated?+

    Eligible orders can be coordinated for consolidation where practical. Supplier locations, cargo compatibility, collection schedules and packaging affect the arrangement.

    Do I need to buy a full container?+

    No. Consolidated cargo may suit smaller orders. The appropriate option depends on the route, volume and type of goods.

    What information do you need for a shipping quote?+

    Start with the origin in China, destination, product type, quantity, packed weight, dimensions and package count. A packing list or supplier invoice helps clarify the cargo.

    Can products be inspected before shipping?+

    Inspection can be included in the agreed service. Confirm the inspection scope before work begins, including the product specifications and checks you need.
