Website terms

Last updated: 13 September 2026

Using this website

This website introduces MZI Shipping and lets visitors prepare sourcing and shipping enquiries. Completing a form does not place an order, create a shipment, take payment or guarantee a service.

Estimates and freight timing

The calculator and freight guide are for planning only. Product type, weight, dimensions, batteries, packing, route availability and clearance requirements can change the price and timeline. Confirm the written quotation before making a purchase.

Service enquiries

Supplier coordination, inspection, shipment acceptance and destination requirements need individual review. A request is not a confirmed quotation or service agreement.

Optional SMS messages

You may choose separate consent for shipment updates and marketing text messages. Marketing messages are limited to four a month. Consent is not a condition of service. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for support. Message and data rates may apply.

Product information

Provide accurate product, supplier, quantity and packing details. Batteries, regulated goods, vehicles and machinery may require more information and may not be accepted for every route.

Business terms

Final commercial terms, company registration details and dispute arrangements are confirmed separately before a customer enters a service agreement.
