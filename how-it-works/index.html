YOUR ORDER STARTS HEREChina → Your destination
  1. 01

    Tell us what you need

    Send a description, specifications, a photo or a supplier link. Let us know the quantity and destination.

  2. 02

    Confirm the product and supplier

    We review the product, supplier and shipping requirements with you before agreeing on the next step.

  3. 03

    Review your quotation

    Check the costs, shipping method and agreed services before the order proceeds.

  4. 04

    Prepare the goods in China

    Depending on your order, we coordinate collection, inspection, consolidation and export preparation.

  5. 05

    Move your shipment

    Air, sea or another suitable freight method is arranged for the agreed route and cargo.

  6. 06

    Follow the important stages

    Receive updates as your goods move through the major stages of the shipment.
