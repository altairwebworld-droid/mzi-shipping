    Good questions.
    Clear answers.

    All frequently asked questions ↗
    Can you help me find a supplier in China?+

    Yes. Send the product description, quantity, destination and any specifications or photos. We can help identify suitable sourcing options and coordinate the next steps.

    Can I send an Alibaba or 1688 link?+

    Yes. You can share Alibaba, 1688, Taobao, Made-in-China or a supplier website link. Include the product options and quantity you want so the enquiry is clear.

    Can you ship machinery?+

    We can help with machinery sourcing and shipping enquiries. Share the application, output, electrical requirements, dimensions and weight. Handling and freight options depend on the machine.
