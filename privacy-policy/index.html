Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 September 2026

Information you provide

The website lets you prepare a local enquiry summary with contact, product and shipping details. It does not currently send the form or upload selected files. Your browser may offer autofill or form restoration.

Mobile numbers and messages

MZI Shipping does not sell or share mobile numbers with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional use. If you opt in, MZI Shipping may send shipment updates or up to four marketing text messages a month. Message and data rates may apply.

Your choices

SMS consent is optional and is not required to request a quote or service. You can opt out of messages by replying STOP. Reply HELP for support with the messaging service.

Files and downloads

Selected photos and documents stay on your device. A downloaded request summary contains file names, not file contents. You choose where to store or share that download.

Hosting

The website host may process technical information needed to deliver the site, including network and access information. This website does not use marketing analytics.

Questions

Use the MZI Shipping contact channel supplied with your order. Direct contact details will be added to the contact page when confirmed.
